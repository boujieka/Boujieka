# Book 7 positioning note

## Title
**Hydropower Development and Finance**
*From River to Financial Close: A Developer, Lender and Government Framework for Hydropower Projects in Africa*

## Cover line under the subtitle
**The Hydro Readiness Framework™: 8 Questions · 23 Gates · 1 Financial Close Decision**

## Official positioning sentence
The IFC guide explains how a hydro project is developed; this book explains how to decide whether it should continue, how it should be financed, who should bear each risk and whether it is ready to close.

## One-line positioning
The investment committee guide to developing and financing hydropower projects in Africa: one evidence-based route from the river to financial close, tested from the positions of the developer, the lenders and the state.

## Back cover text (draft)
Most hydropower projects in Africa that never reach financial close do not fail on engineering. They fail because the developer, the lenders or the state cannot accept the terms the other two need.

This book is written for the people who make those decisions. It reads a hydro project as three investments in one asset: a development option, a construction contract and an operating annuity. It separates eight questions that are often confused: is the project technically viable, developable, economic, investable, bankable, buildable, affordable, and ready to close? Through the Hydro Readiness Framework, it turns those questions into 23 evidence gates and one close decision: STOP, NOT READY, CONDITIONAL GO or GO.

A companion workbook, MODEL 7, puts every step into numbers, from the flow record and the development budget to debt sizing, the developer's risk-weighted returns, the buyer's capacity to pay and the state's contingent liabilities. A fictional 60 MW run-of-river case, Kasiri River Hydro, runs through every chapter and ends with a recommendation to a co-developer and a group of development finance lenders.

Written for developers, lenders, development finance institutions, investors and government decision-makers, with questions for the investment and credit committees at the end of every chapter, and a technical reference on hydrology, civil works, equipment, construction, operation and E&S due diligence.

Not a hydropower engineering textbook. Not a general project finance book. Not a feasibility study guide. A decision framework for whether a hydro project should proceed, who should fund it, who should bear its risks, and whether it is actually ready to close.

|---|---|---|
| IFC (2015), Hydroelectric Power: A Guide for Developers and Investors | Full development cycle; detailed technical sections | Its own words: financing and permitting are "a high-level review". Book 7 makes the continue, finance and close decisions |

## Series pairing
*Bankable Is Not Enough* sets out why bankability alone can produce poor power deals. Book 7 applies that argument to one sector and turns it into a method: how to decide whether a hydropower project is worth developing, financing and closing.
