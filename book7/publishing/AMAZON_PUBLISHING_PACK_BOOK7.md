## 1. Positioning

### 1.1 Core promise
The practical investment committee framework for developing and financing hydropower projects in Africa: whether a project should keep being developed, who should fund the next stage, who should carry each risk, and whether it is ready for financial close.

### 1.2 Official positioning sentence (from the Preface)
The IFC guide explains how a hydro project is developed; this book explains how to decide whether it should continue, how it should be financed, who should bear each risk and whether it is ready to close.

### 1.3 Claims to avoid
| Claim | Why not |
|---|---|
| "The first book on hydropower finance" | The Hydro Finance Handbook (2008) and the IFC guide (2015) exist; the book itself says so |
| "The complete guide to hydropower" | The book is not an engineering text; the IFC guide covers the full cycle |
| "Validated model" or "bank-grade model" | The model is tested in LibreOffice and a second engine; the Excel test is still open |
| Any return, tariff or cost figure presented as a market benchmark | Kasiri figures are illustrative case outputs |
| "Hydro Readiness Framework®" | The ® symbol requires registration; use ™ only after the legal check (D6) |

## 5. Amazon description

About 2,300 characters. KDP accepts a small set of HTML tags in the description (confirm in KDP); the version below uses only bold and lists.

```html
<b>Should this hydropower project keep being developed? Who should fund the next stage? Who should carry each risk? And is it actually ready for financial close?</b>

<p>Most hydropower projects in Africa that never reach financial close do not fail on engineering. They fail because the developer, the lenders or the state cannot accept the terms the other two need. This book is written for the people who make those decisions.</p>

<p><b>Hydropower Development and Finance</b> reads a hydro project as three investments in one asset: a development option, a construction contract and an operating annuity, each with its own risk, capital and way of failing. It follows one chain of analysis from the river to the close: hydrology, configuration, the route to a power purchase agreement, development stages and budget, consents, contracts, construction, operation, debt, the lender group, the developer's returns, the state's obligations and stress testing.</p>

<p>At its centre is the <b>Hydro Readiness Framework™: 8 questions, 23 evidence gates and one financial close decision</b> (STOP, NOT READY, CONDITIONAL GO or GO), with each question assigned to the party that must accept it: developer, lender or government.</p>

<p>Inside the book:</p>
<ul>
<li>Developer economics: risk-weighted development value, success-path return and the price of a co-developer's entry</li>
<li>Debt sizing on a variable river: P50, one-year and ten-year P90, multi-year drought tests and reserves</li>
<li>The buyer and the state: utility payment capacity, guarantees, contingent liabilities and the consolidated fiscal effect</li>
<li>Stress testing with the financing package locked, including construction overruns at reference-class levels</li>
<li>Checklists for the PPA, construction contracts, conditions precedent, lender data requests and the investment memo</li>
<li>A technical due-diligence reference on hydrology, civil works, equipment, construction and E&S</li>
<li>A worked 60 MW case, Kasiri River Hydro, from site to a recommendation to a co-developer and a lender group</li>
</ul>

<p>Every number in the case comes from a companion financial model, and every external claim is sourced, with its verification status recorded.</p>

<p>For developers, lenders, development finance institutions, investors and government decision-makers, with questions for the investment and credit committees at the end of every chapter.</p>

<p>Book 7 of the Africa Energy Finance collection.</p>
```
