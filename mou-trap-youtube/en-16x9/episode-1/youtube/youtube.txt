TITLE
The MoU Trap · Ep 1/7: Fourteen contracts, no power

DESCRIPTION
In July 2016, Nigeria's bulk electricity trader signed power purchase agreements with fourteen solar developers: some 1,125 MW at 11.5 US cents per kWh, fixed for 20 years. Three years later, none had reached financial close. Why a signed commitment is not yet a financeable one.

Episode 1 of 7 in "The MoU Trap", a cartoon training series for ministers, cabinets, regulators and other power-sector decision-makers.
