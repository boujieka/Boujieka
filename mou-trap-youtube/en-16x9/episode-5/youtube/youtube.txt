The MoU Trap · Ep 5/7: When the deal closes anyway

DESCRIPTION
Ghana signed 43 PPAs through non-competitive processes during and after a supply crisis; in 2019 net sector arrears stood at US$2.75 billion. Then the fair case for MoUs, and Cameroon's Nachtigal hydropower plant: well structured, and still exposed to a weak buyer.
