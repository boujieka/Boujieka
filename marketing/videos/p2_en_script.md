# Narration script: Energy Access Financial Model, Developer Edition

Length: 4 min 20 s. One line per subtitle, with its timing.
To record a voice-over, read each sentence while it is shown on screen.

## 00:00:00  How to use the model

* `00:01` How to use the Energy Access Financial Model, Developer Edition.
* `00:06` By Emmanuel Boujieka Kamga.

## 00:00:10  Step 1 of 14: What the model answers

* `00:10` The Developer Edition follows a mini-grid project from community demand to the financing request.
* `00:18` It tells you whether the project can become bankable, and with what mix of grant, RBF, concessional debt, senior debt and equity.

## 00:00:29  Step 2 of 14: Project and scenarios

* `00:29` On the Inputs sheet, start with the project, rates and tax, then choose the active scenario from the list.
* `00:38` Scenarios model out-turn risk on a system already built: the sizing stays on the base case.

## 00:00:46  Step 3 of 14: Segments, RBF and affordability

* `00:46` Each segment has its own tariff, connection fee, RBF amount and monthly income.
* `00:53` The model compares the year one bill with your affordability threshold and flags any segment above it.

## 00:01:02  Step 4 of 14: Hourly load profile

* `01:02` The load profile gives each segment's share of daily energy, hour by hour.
* `01:08` The night-time share sizes the battery, and the peak ratio sizes the diesel generator. Replace the illustrative shapes with metered data when you have it.

## 00:01:21  Step 5 of 14: Productive uses

* `01:21` List the productive appliances expected in the community.
* `01:26` Their monthly consumption, divided by the number of productive users, can replace the direct entry on the Inputs sheet.

## 00:01:36  Step 6 of 14: Funding structure

* `01:37` Set the capital grant, the two debt tranches with their rates, tenors and grace periods, and the debt service reserve.
* `01:46` Equity covers the rest, including the initial DSRA.

## 00:01:51  Step 7 of 14: Dashboard and verdicts

* `01:52` The dashboard gives project and equity returns, DSCR and LCOE, six verdicts and affordability by segment.
* `02:00` The equity verdict uses NPV at the target rate, because IRR can mislead when cash flows change sign.

## 00:02:09  Step 8 of 14: Size of the gap

* `02:09` The Funding Gap sheet is the core of the model. It first sizes the viability gap, then the grant needed given the RBF you plan.
* `02:19` Cash flows are linear in RBF, so these results are exact and need no Goal Seek.

## 00:02:26  Step 9 of 14: RBF needed and debt capacity

* `02:27` The model gives the uniform RBF per connection for a zero project NPV, and for the equity target return.
* `02:35` In the example, equity reaches its target with 347 dollars per connection instead of 260.
* `02:42` It also computes the largest senior loan that keeps DSCR above the minimum every year, and names the binding year.

## 00:02:52  Step 10 of 14: Live sensitivity

* `02:52` Each variable moves up and down by twenty percent, one at a time, and the tornado ranks them by their effect on NPV.
* `03:01` Note the demand result: with a system sized for base demand, extra kWh come from diesel, and NPV falls.

## 00:03:10  Step 11 of 14: Impact and MRV

* `03:11` The Impact and MRV sheet tracks verified connections, people with access, jobs, energy, CO2 avoided and RBF disbursed, year by year, with cost-effectiveness ratios.

## 00:03:24  Step 12 of 14: Financing request

* `03:25` The Financing Request sheet writes six paragraphs from your results, with sources and uses and key metrics.
* `03:33` Copy them into your concept note, then review and adapt the wording.

## 00:03:40  Step 13 of 14: Sixteen integrity checks

* `03:40` Sixteen checks run at every recalculation, including identity checks that confirm the RBF calibration. Use results only when the sheet reads ALL OK.

## 00:03:52  Step 14 of 14: The PDF manual

* `03:53` The sixteen-page manual explains every method, the worked example and the limits of use. Read the limits before presenting a result to a committee.

## 00:04:05  What you receive

* `04:06` You receive the workbook in English and French, the PDF manual and the integrity checks.
* `04:13` The example values are illustrative: replace them with the data of your own project.
