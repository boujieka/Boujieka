# Narration script: Mini-Grid Feasibility Calculator

Length: 4 min 01 s. One line per subtitle, with its timing.
To record a voice-over, read each sentence while it is shown on screen.

## 00:00:00  How to use the model

* `00:01` How to use the Mini-Grid Feasibility Calculator.
* `00:05` By Emmanuel Boujieka Kamga.

## 00:00:09  Step 1 of 12: What the calculator answers

* `00:09` This calculator assesses a hybrid solar, battery and diesel mini-grid at pre-feasibility stage.
* `00:17` It answers three questions: is the project viable on tariff revenue alone, can it carry debt, and how much subsidy closes the gap?

## 00:00:28  Step 2 of 12: Inputs: project and finance

* `00:28` Open the Inputs sheet. You only edit the yellow cells with blue text; every other cell is a formula.
* `00:36` Start with the project: life, hurdle rate, equity target, minimum DSCR, escalation and tax.
* `00:43` Keep the hurdle rate and the escalation on the same basis, both nominal.

## 00:00:50  Step 3 of 12: Scenario levers

* `00:50` Four multipliers stress the tariff, demand, CAPEX and OPEX.
* `00:55` A value of 1.00 keeps your inputs as entered; 0.85 on demand simulates consumption fifteen percent below forecast.

## 00:01:05  Step 4 of 12: Customers and demand

* `01:06` Enter each customer segment: number of customers, monthly consumption, tariff and connection fee.
* `01:13` Then set the connection ramp-up, the growth in consumption and the collection rate.
* `01:20` Use survey or metering data rather than an appliance list, which usually overstates early demand.

## 00:01:29  Step 5 of 12: Technical design and sizing

* `01:29` The calculator sizes PV, battery and diesel from energy sold in year three.
* `01:35` Solar energy used at night passes through the battery, so round-trip losses raise the PV required.
* `01:43` If you have a design, type your values in the Override column.

## 00:01:49  Step 6 of 12: CAPEX and OPEX

* `01:50` Enter unit costs for PV, storage, generator, network and meters, then the operating costs.
* `01:57` Battery replacements can be smoothed through a maintenance reserve, which keeps the DSCR stable in replacement years.

## 00:02:07  Step 7 of 12: Financing and subsidies

* `02:07` Describe the financing: upfront grant, RBF per new connection with its verification lag, and the senior loan terms.
* `02:16` RBF arrives after commissioning and verification, so the developer has to bridge it.

## 00:02:24  Step 8 of 12: Reading the dashboard

* `02:24` The dashboard shows project IRR and NPV before and after subsidies, the LCOE against levelized collected revenue, the DSCR and equity returns.
* `02:35` Compare the LCOE with collected revenue, not with the year one tariff: both are discounted the same way.

## 00:02:44  Step 9 of 12: Viability gap and verdicts

* `02:45` The viability gap is the upfront subsidy that brings pre-subsidy NPV to zero at your hurdle rate.
* `02:52` In the example it reaches 907 dollars per connection, and the planned grant and RBF just close it.
* `03:00` Five verdicts summarise bankability. Here the debt covenant is met, but equity returns stay below target.

## 00:03:09  Step 10 of 12: Twenty years at a glance

* `03:10` The charts show twenty years of revenue, OPEX and CFADS against debt service, and the split between solar and diesel generation.

## 00:03:21  Step 11 of 12: Integrity checks

* `03:21` Before using any result, open the Checks sheet. Every line must read OK, and the dashboard must show ALL OK.

## 00:03:30  Step 12 of 12: The PDF manual
