# Narration script: Energy Access Fund Manager Model, RBF & Portfolio Edition

Length: 3 min 54 s. One line per subtitle, with its timing.
To record a voice-over, read each sentence while it is shown on screen.

## 00:00:00  How to use the model

* `00:01` How to use the Energy Access Fund Manager Model, RBF & Portfolio Edition.
* `00:07` By Emmanuel Boujieka Kamga.

## 00:00:10  Step 1 of 13: What the model answers

* `00:11` The Fund Manager Edition helps the manager of a grant or RBF window decide which projects to support, with how much, and what portfolio that buys.

## 00:00:23  Step 2 of 13: Envelope and limits

* `00:24` Start with the fund: its size, management and technical assistance costs, the over-commitment ratio, and the concentration limits per project and per country.
* `00:36` Over-commitment is prudent only if expected under-delivery covers it.

## 00:00:42  Step 3 of 13: Support profile

* `00:43` Choose a support profile: RBF per connection by customer type, capital grant share, caps and RBF tranches.
* `00:51` Use the Custom profile to mirror the published rules of your programme.

## 00:00:58  Step 4 of 13: Eligibility rules

* `00:58` Eight eligibility criteria can be switched on or off: size, unit cost, renewable share, co-finance, readiness, tariff, productive use and country.

## 00:01:10  Step 5 of 13: Weights and delivery

* `01:11` Set the scoring weights, which must total one hundred percent, ideally before applications arrive.
* `01:19` The delivery table links each risk rating to the share of connections you expect to see delivered.

## 00:01:27  Step 6 of 13: Applicant pipeline

* `01:28` Enter one row per applicant. You can paste CAPEX, viability gap and CO2 from the Developer Edition; the viability gap feeds the additionality test.

## 00:01:40  Step 7 of 13: Eligibility with reasons

* `01:40` Each project is tested against every active criterion. A rejected project shows the first criterion it fails.
* `01:49` In the example, eight of the fourteen applicants are eligible.

## 00:01:55  Step 8 of 13: Allocation in rank order

* `01:56` Eligible projects are funded in score order. Each row looks only at the rows above it, so the envelope and country limits apply without circular references.
* `02:07` Here project N is limited by the country ceiling, and project B by the end of the envelope.

## 00:02:15  Step 9 of 13: Disbursements by year

* `02:16` Grants are paid at commissioning. RBF is paid in two tranches as connections are verified, reduced by the expected delivery rate.
* `02:26` The fund cash position then shows whether commitments stay within available funds.

## 00:02:33  Step 10 of 13: MRV tracker

* `02:34` During the life of the fund, enter verified connections and payments to date. The tracker compares progress with the expected profile and gives each project a status.

## 00:02:48  Step 11 of 13: Portfolio dashboard

* `02:48` The dashboard sums up the portfolio: 2.55 million dollars allocated, about 7,960 connections funded, private capital mobilised, cost per connection, concentration and four verdicts.
* `03:02` The over-commitment indicator shows that this grant-heavy portfolio can only absorb an over-commitment of about 1.02.

## 00:03:12  Step 12 of 13: Twelve integrity checks

* `03:12` Twelve checks confirm the weights, the limits and the disbursement schedule. Use the results only when the sheet reads ALL OK.

## 00:03:23  Step 13 of 13: The PDF manual

* `03:24` The fifteen-page manual explains the method, the scoring, the allocation rules, the worked example and the limits of use. The model supports decisions; it does not replace an investment committee.

## 00:03:39  What you receive

* `03:40` You receive the workbook in English and French, the PDF manual and the integrity checks.
* `03:47` The example values are illustrative: replace them with the data of your own project.
