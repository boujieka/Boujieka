**Profile page text (section above the products)**
```
When the tariff people can pay does not cover the cost of the kWh, a mini-grid needs subsidy. The hard part is sizing it: how much, in what form, and can the project still repay its debt?

These models answer that question in Excel, with readable formulas and no macros.

START HERE
Mini-Grid Financial Feasibility Calculator. A first answer in about ten minutes: IRR, NPV, LCOE, DSCR and the viability gap.

FOR DEVELOPERS
Energy Access Project Financial Model, Developer Edition. Exact calibration of the grant and RBF per connection, affordability by customer segment, two debt tranches, live sensitivity, impact and MRV indicators, and a financing note written by the model.

FOR FUNDS AND PROGRAMMES
Energy Access Fund Manager Model, RBF & Portfolio Edition. Eligibility screening, project scoring, allocation of a grant or RBF envelope within project and country limits, disbursements and MRV tracking.

EVERY MODEL INCLUDES
English and French versions. A PDF user manual of 15 to 16 pages with the method, formulas, a worked example and the limits of use. Integrity checks built into the workbook. Excel 2010 or later, and LibreOffice.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR
Emmanuel Boujieka Kamga, climate and energy-access finance specialist. More than twenty years across 21 African markets, results-based and viability-gap instruments designed for national mini-grid programmes, and more than USD 1 billion of renewable energy finance co-structured. Engineer by training, MBA from HEC Montréal.

These are screening and pre-feasibility tools. They do not replace engineering design, due diligence or professional advice. Example figures are illustrative.

Questions about fit with your programme's rules? Write before you buy.
```
