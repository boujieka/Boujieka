# Etsy shop description

Two fields carry the shop description on Etsy: the **shop title** (one line under the shop name) and the **shop announcement** (the text at the top of the shop page). The "About" story is in `ETSY_ABOUT.md`.
Etsy's current character limits are not verified here: check them in Shop Manager. The titles below are kept under 55 characters as a precaution.

---

```
Excel financial models for mini-grids and energy access
```

```
Excel financial models for energy-access projects: solar mini-grids, results-based financing (RBF) and subsidy funds.
```

## Points to settle before publishing

* Refund policy for digital files: Etsy lets each seller set it. Decide and state it in the shop policies, not here.
* Prices are deliberately left out of the announcement so that a price test does not require editing the shop text.
