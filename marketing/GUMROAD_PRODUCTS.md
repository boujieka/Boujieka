# 1. Mini-Grid Financial Feasibility Calculator

## English

**Name**
```
Mini-Grid Financial Feasibility Calculator (Excel, EN + FR)
```

**Price:** USD 24

**Description**
```
Is your mini-grid viable, can it carry debt, and how much subsidy does it really need?

This Excel calculator gives a first answer in about ten minutes. It is built for energy-access projects (rural mini-grids with solar PV, batteries and diesel backup), not for utility-scale solar farms.

WHAT IT CALCULATES
Project IRR and NPV, before and after subsidies
LCOE compared with the revenue you actually collect
DSCR, minimum and average, against your lender's covenant
Equity IRR and payback
The viability gap: the upfront subsidy that brings the project to your hurdle rate, and the share your planned grant and RBF already cover

HOW IT WORKS
Five customer segments (households, productive users, commercial, institutions, custom), with connection ramp-up and collection rate.
Automatic sizing of PV, battery and diesel generator, including battery round-trip losses, with a manual override column.
Battery replacements smoothed through a maintenance reserve, as lenders expect.
Upfront grant and RBF per verified connection, with a verification lag.
Scenario levers on tariff, demand, CAPEX and OPEX.

WORKED EXAMPLE INCLUDED
A fictitious village of 970 customers. Without subsidy the project shows a viability gap of USD 907 per connection. With the planned grant and RBF, NPV turns positive and the minimum DSCR reaches 1.55x, while equity IRR stays below target. You see every output before entering your own data.

WHAT IT IS NOT
A screening and pre-feasibility tool. It does not replace an hourly simulation, engineering design or due diligence. It does not model foreign exchange, VAT or working capital. The manual lists every simplification.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR
Emmanuel Boujieka Kamga, climate and energy-access finance specialist with more than twenty years across 21 African markets. MBA, HEC Montréal.
```

# 2. Energy Access Project Financial Model, Developer Edition

## English

**Name**
```
Energy Access Project Financial Model, Developer Edition (Excel, EN + FR)
```

**Price:** USD 99

**Description**
```
How much grant or results-based financing does your mini-grid really need, and with what debt? This model answers exactly, from community demand to a financing request.

FOR WHOM
Mini-grid developers applying to RBF or grant programmes, energy-access consultants, and programme teams who size subsidies.

THE FUNDING GAP, SOLVED IN CLOSED FORM
Viability gap at your hurdle rate
Grant needed, given your planned RBF
RBF per connection needed for project NPV = 0, and for your equity target return
Maximum senior debt at your minimum DSCR, and the year that binds
RBF cash timing, and the bridge you need before it arrives
No Goal Seek: cash flows are linear in RBF, so the results are exact. Built-in identity checks confirm it at every recalculation.

DEMAND AND DESIGN
Five customer segments, each with its own tariff, connection fee, RBF amount and monthly income, with an affordability test.
Hourly load profile per segment: the night-time share sizes the battery and the peak ratio sizes the generator.
Productive-use inventory (mills, welding, cold rooms, pumps) that can drive productive users' consumption.

FINANCE
Capital grant, RBF, concessional debt, senior debt, equity and DSRA. Maintenance reserve for battery replacements. Tax loss carry-forward.
Scenarios (Base, Conservative, Optimistic, Custom) and a live tornado on tariff, demand, CAPEX, OPEX, collection, diesel price and interest rates.

IMPACT AND REPORTING
Impact & MRV sheet: verified connections, people with access, productive users, jobs, MWh, CO2 avoided, RBF disbursed, grant per connection and per tCO2, private capital leverage.
Financing Request sheet: six paragraphs written from your results, sources and uses, key metrics, ready to adapt for a concept note.

WORKED EXAMPLE INCLUDED
970 customers. The planned grant and RBF close the gap at project level and the debt meets its covenant, but equity falls short of its target. The model shows that RBF would need to rise from USD 260 to USD 347 per connection. It also shows a less obvious result: 20% more demand lowers NPV, because a system sized for base demand serves the extra kWh with diesel.

WHAT IT IS NOT
No foreign exchange, VAT, working capital, balance sheet or sculpted debt. It focuses on subsidy sizing, affordability and impact, and works alongside a full project-finance model. The manual lists every simplification.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR
Emmanuel Boujieka Kamga, climate and energy-access finance specialist. More than twenty years across 21 African markets, results-based and viability-gap instruments designed for national mini-grid programmes. MBA, HEC Montréal.
```

Fund managers, programme teams, development finance institutions, foundations and consultants who run or design RBF and grant windows for mini-grids and energy access.

FROM CALL FOR PROPOSALS TO MRV
Fund set-up: size, management and technical assistance costs, over-commitment ratio, per-project and per-country limits.

MRV tracker: verified connections against targets, RBF earned and outstanding, On track, Behind or Off track.
Portfolio dashboard: connections, people with access, CO2 avoided, private capital mobilised, cost per connection, concentration and four verdicts.

WORKS WITH THE DEVELOPER EDITION
Paste each project's CAPEX, viability gap and CO2 from the Developer Edition to test additionality: does the fund pay more than the project needs?

A fictitious USD 3 million fund and 14 applicants. Eight are eligible, six are rejected with their reason, and USD 2.55 million is allocated across eight projects within a 50% country ceiling. The manual also shows why over-committing a grant-heavy envelope breaks the fund's cash position.
