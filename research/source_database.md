# Bankable Hydro - Research / Source Database

Research date: 2026-10-03. Companion CSV: `research/source_database.csv` (same catalogue, columns id, theme, title, publisher, year, url, doc_type, relevance).

## Methodology

- **Scope.** Primary and institutional sources (IMF, World Bank Group incl. IFC/MIGA/ESMAP/PPIAF, AfDB, IRENA, IEA, IHA, regional power pools, peer-reviewed literature) relevant to structuring African hydropower without unsustainable public liabilities. Seven themes as tabulated below. News/secondary items are included only where they document an event (e.g., the 2024 Kariba drought) or relay a primary source not directly accessible, and are labelled as such.
- **Search and access.** Web search (Oct 2026) followed by direct retrieval. Where the full document could be downloaded, it was parsed (pdftotext) and the benchmark numbers were read from the text/tables themselves. Several hosts (imf.org, cldp.doc.gov, ppiaf.org PDFs, sciencedirect) blocked automated retrieval; for those, only the bibliographic record and search-result snippet were seen.
- **Verification flag (column V).** `F` = full text opened and benchmark numbers read by the analyst; `P` = landing/summary page opened; `S` = located via search results only (title/URL/year confirmed from search metadata; any numbers marked 'via search snippet' should be re-checked against the full text before publication).
- **No fabrication rule.** Only documents actually located are listed, with the URL exactly as seen. Where a year or detail could not be confirmed it is shown as 'n.d.' or annotated. Analyst-derived figures (e.g., USD/km computed from a project table, USD/kW computed from a PAD) are explicitly marked 'analyst calc.'.
- **Benchmark use.** The ranges at the end are intended as *model defaults* only; each must be overridden with project-specific data. Ranges combine global reference classes (e.g., Ansar et al.) with African observations (IRENA Africa splits, Nachtigal PAD, EAPP/SAPP plans). Currency years differ by source and are stated in the Unit column.
- **Known gaps.** (1) No institutional source located that states *typical DSCR covenant levels* for African hydro IPPs; only the Nachtigal lenders' base case (1.45x min / 1.53x avg) is documented - treat DSCR covenants as user inputs. (2) The World Bank 'Linking Up' report PDF could not be opened; its transmission cost figures were not extracted. (3) IMF country reports could not be downloaded; Ethiopia, Ghana, Malawi, Uganda facts rely on search snippets of imf.org pages (flag S). (4) No dedicated World Bank 'Hydropower PPP Toolkit' was found; the closest are HY-06 (ESMAP 2024) and CL-06 (Climate Toolkits for Infrastructure PPPs - Hydropower).
