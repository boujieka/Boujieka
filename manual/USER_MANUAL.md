# BANKABLE HYDRO: Integrated Bankability Model
## User Manual, v1.0

File: `model/Bankable_Hydro_Model.xlsx` (generated by `model/build_model.py`)

---

## 1. What the model does

The model answers one question:

**Can this hydropower project deliver bankable power without creating unsustainable public liabilities?**

It does this by following one engine through five layers:

1. Project
2. Power system
3. Offtaker / utility
4. Regulation / PPP
5. Sovereign / public finance

The engine is annual, covers 40 periods and contains about 9,800 live formulas. It has:

- no macros;
- no circular references;
- no data tables.

The reference project, *Lumora Falls* in the *Republic of Navaria*, is **fictional**. Replace every blue input before using the model on a real project.

## 4. Quick start (15 minutes)

1. Open `01_CONTROL_PANEL`. Keep the defaults: Base case, P50 cash flows, P90 lender case, Structure 3 (PPP), debt sculpted, backstop on, no stresses.
2. Open `32_DASHBOARD` and read the verdict line, the gate column and the top 5 gaps.
3. Switch on **Offtaker stress** (`01_CONTROL_PANEL`, set it to 1). Watch:
   - Utility payment capacity falls below 1x.
   - Lifetime offtaker gap rises.
   - Fiscal NPV turns sharply negative.
   - Gate 5 moves to CRITICAL GAP.
4. Set **Government budget backstop** to 0. The shortfall now becomes a PPA guarantee call (in structure 3) or, in a structure without a PPA guarantee, unpaid arrears that hit project DSCR.
5. Change **Structure** from 1 to 5 and compare `17A_STRUCTURES`.
6. Check that `33_CHECKS` shows **ALL OK**.

## 5. Workflow by user

### 5.1 Developer / sponsor

1. Calibrate `03`, `05`, `07` and `14` to the feasibility study and the term sheet.
2. Set `gen_case` = 1 (P50) and `lender_case` = 3 (P90).
3. Read private equity IRR against the target IRR (`17A`), and the financing gap (`17`).
4. Use `fx_tariff` to find the tariff that closes the gap. Then check affordability in `12` and `15`.

### 5.2 Lender / DFI

1. Size the debt in mode 1 and note `debt_m` (`17_PROJECT_FINANCE`).
2. Paste that figure into `lock_m` (`18_DEBT`) and set `debt_mode` = 2.
3. Run the stresses one at a time, then combined. Read minimum DSCR, LLCR and the DS shortfall.
4. Focus on Gates 1, 4, 5 and 7.

### 5.3 Ministry of Finance / PPP unit

1. Replace the country inputs in `26_DEBT_SUSTAINABILITY` with the latest published DSA data.
2. Set the screening thresholds to your fiscal-risk policy.
3. Enter your own call probabilities in `24`. They are judgements, not forecasts.
4. Read `22`–`26`, and the snapshot in `27`, for the worst plausible fiscal case.
5. Use the `17A` structure comparison to compare forms of exposure.
6. Export results into PFRAM for the formal assessment.

### 5.4 Utility

1. Replace `12_UTILITY` inputs with audited accounts: sales, tariff, losses, collection, costs, transfers and debt service.
2. Read maximum sustainable PPA payment against the PPA bill, and the affordability headroom (`15`).

### 5.5 Transaction adviser

1. Run all five structures with the snapshot runner (section 8).
2. Use `29` and `30` to build the pre-close action plan.

## 6. Calculation methodology (key formulas)

**Hydrology (`03`)**
- Usable flow = MIN(MAX(Q − e-flow, 0), Q_design).
- Power = MIN(ρgQHη/10⁶, P_installed).
- Energy = Power × hours × availability.
- P90 = P50(1 − 1.2816·CV). P75 = P50(1 − 0.6745·CV).

**Generation (`04`)**
- Generation = P_selected × case flow factor × drought factor × climate factor × ramp.
- Evacuable = Generation × MIN(1, evac MW / installed MW).
- Delivered = MIN(Evacuable, absorbable demand).

**Revenue (`16`)**
- Capacity payment = capacity charge × MW × 12 / 1000 × availability ratio × ramp.
- Energy payment = energy charge × delivered / 1000.
- Deemed energy = curtailment × deemed flag.
- Cash revenue = billed − unpaid.

**Utility (`12`)**
- Utility demand excludes the mining load the project serves directly. Other supply is capped at the energy available (`d_sup`); demand above the cap is unserved.
- Cash available before PPA = collections + transfers − OPEX − other supply − existing debt service.
- No-project counterfactual (`u_cash_np`) and incremental utility cash `f_soe` = cash after PPA − no-project cash − new arrears.
- Max sustainable PPA = MAX(0, cash) / coverage.
- Gap = MAX(0, PPA bill − max sustainable).

**Shortfall routing (`16C`)**
- Backstop = gap × backstop share, if backstop = 1.
- Guarantee call = MIN(remaining gap, guarantee limit − calls outstanding + reimbursements). The limit is `ppag_months` of utility billing; the utility reimburses calls from spare payment capacity.
- Unpaid = remainder (arrears to the project, which reduce cash revenue).

**Debt (`18`)**
- Sculpted DS_t = MAX(0, CFADS_lender,t / DSCR_target − concessional DS_t).
- Capacity = Σ DS_t / (1 + r)^opyr.
- Commercial debt = MIN(capacity, gearing-limited amount), where the gearing limit applies to CAPEX plus debt-funded IDC and fees through a closed-form IDC factor (no circularity).
- In locked mode (`debt_mode` = 2), every tranche amount, the grant, government equity and the commercial principal schedule are held at base-case values (written by the snapshot runner). Interest floats only on the unhedged share (`hedge`).

**DSRA**
- Target = DSRA months / 12 × next-year debt service. The initial balance is equity-funded at the end of construction.
- The reserve is drawn first when cash falls short, releases only the excess over target, and is topped up only from available cash.

**Waterfall (`20`)**
- CFADS = EBITDA − tax.
- Shortfall = MAX(0, −(CFADS − DS + DSRA release + trapped cash)).
- The shortfall is funded by the sovereign guarantee (guaranteed share) and by sponsors. The sovereign's claim is repaid from later cash ahead of distributions.
- Distributions are paid only if DSCR ≥ lock-up.

**LCOE (`17`)**
- Plant LCOE = NPV(plant costs) / NPV(delivered + deemed energy).
- System LCOE adds public transmission CAPEX and O&M, and uses energy net of transmission losses.

**Contingent liabilities (`24`)**
- Maximum simultaneous exposure = MAX(termination, debt guarantee + PPA guarantee + FX cover).
- Termination = debt + MAX(unrecovered equity × (1 + premium), PV of remaining private distributions at target IRR).
- Expected loss = Σ probability × exposure × LGD.

**Fiscal (`25`)**
- Net fiscal cash flow (central government) = −direct support − calls + tax + royalty + government dividends + recoveries of calls.
- Consolidated fiscal NPV adds the state utility's incremental cash (`f_soe`). Use it as the headline public-sector measure.

**Screening (`26`)** uses four tests:
1. On-budget increment to debt/GDP.
2. Contingent exposure / GDP.
3. Annual cash need / revenue.
4. The project pushes debt above the benchmark.

The result combines these with the DSA rating to give LOW, MODERATE or HIGH.

**Bankability (`30`)**
- Test score: 3 if the metric meets the READY threshold, 2 if CONDITIONAL, 1 if DEVELOPMENT GAP, otherwise 0. The direction is H (higher is better) or L (lower is better).
- Gate score = MIN of its test scores.
- Overall verdict follows the rule in the book (Chapter 16).

## 7. Scenarios and stresses

**Cases** (choose one): Base, Low, High. Each sets the flow, CAPEX, demand-growth and OPEX factors (`27_SCENARIOS`).

**Stresses** (combinable; parameters in `27_SCENARIOS`):

| Toggle | Default stress | Source / note |
|---|---|---|
| Drought | Flows × 0.55 for 3 op-years from year 4 | Calibrate to the worst historical sequence. Kariba 2024 context in the source DB |
| CAPEX overrun | +27% | Ansar et al. 2014 median [SD HY-08]. The mean was +96% |
| Construction delay | +2 years, plus 4% cost per year | Ansar et al. mean schedule overrun 2.3 years [SD HY-08] |
| Low demand | Demand × 0.80 | – |
| Offtaker stress | Collections −8 pp, transfers −30%, retail tariff frozen for 5 years from COD | Fictional |
| (snapshot only) CAPEX overrun at reference-class mean | +96% | Ansar et al. 2014 mean [SD HY-08] |
| FX | One-off 50% step devaluation at COD | Fictional |
| High interest rate | +300 bp on commercial debt | Concessional debt is fixed-rate |
| Transmission delay | Line 2 years late | – |
| Climate trend | −3% mean flow per decade | Illustrative; needs a basin study |

**Flexes** (CAPEX, generation, tariff, OPEX, rate) are applied on top of the case and stresses.

## 8. Refreshing snapshots (full-engine scenario, structure and sensitivity tables)

```bash
python model/build_model.py                                    # rebuild the workbook from code (optional)
python <path>/recalc.py model/Bankable_Hydro_Model.xlsx 200   # LibreOffice recalculation; or open and save in Excel
RECALC=<path>/recalc.py python tools/run_snapshots.py         # 31 full-engine cases + tariff solves
```

The runner copies the model, sets the control inputs, recalculates in LibreOffice, reads the KPIs and writes dated static tables into `27`, `17A` and `28`.

It runs stress cases with the financing package **locked** at base-case terms (tranche amounts, grant, government equity and the commercial principal schedule), as a lender would. It also solves, for structures 2 to 5, the tariff that gives the target equity IRR.

If you edit inputs directly in the workbook rather than in `build_model.py`, run only `run_snapshots.py`.

## 9. Integrity checks (`33_CHECKS`)

The 14 checks are:

1. Sources = uses.
2. Annual construction funding balances.
3. CAPEX phasing = 100%.
4. Concessional debt fully repaid.
5. Commercial debt fully repaid.
6. Concessional term within the concession.
7. Commercial tenor within the concession.
8. Horizon sufficient.
9. Delivered ≤ generated.
10. Unpaid ≥ 0.
11. No forced balloon on commercial debt.
12. DSRA never negative.
13. Project-funded transmission fully funded.
14. Structure funding coherent.

**Never use outputs unless `ALL OK` is shown.**

## 11. Known simplifications

- **Annual periodicity.** No intra-year dispatch. Curtailment is proportional to evacuation shortfall.
- **P-values.** Normal approximation from CV.
- **Drawdown.** Debt is drawn pro rata with CAPEX rather than after equity, which slightly flatters the equity IRR. The initial DSRA is equity-funded.
- **Lender-case tax.** Ignores interest deductibility (conservative). The lender case applies a one-year P90 to every year.
- **FX step.** Permanent in real terms; no pass-through to local prices; receivables are not revalued.
- **Utility model.** Single, simplified cash model with no balance sheet. Opex scales with sales volume. Other-supply cost is half USD-linked.
- **Guarantee calls.** Deterministic within a scenario. Expected values are only as good as the probabilities entered.
- **Termination exposure.** Uses a simplified formula (debt + unrecovered equity × (1 + premium)).
- **Fiscal screen.** Not a debt sustainability analysis.
- **Portfolio effects.** Other PPAs and guarantees held by the same government are not aggregated.

## 12. Rebuilding or extending the model

`model/build_model.py` is the single source of truth. Each formula is written once as a template:

- `{name}` = a scalar cell;
- `[name]` = the same column of a time-series row;
- `[name@p]` / `[name@n]` = previous / next column;
- `[RNG:name]` = full row range.

Templates are resolved after all sheets are built. Name collisions are blocked by assertions. A cycle detector was used in development to guarantee no circular references.

To add a line, add an `inp()`, `calc()` or `ts()` call. Then rebuild, recalculate and confirm `33_CHECKS`.
