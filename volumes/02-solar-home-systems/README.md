# Volume 2 — Solar Home Systems: PAYGo Business & Financial Model

**Central question:** *Can PAYGo solar become profitable and financeable?*

**Core thesis:** a PAYGo SHS company is three businesses in one: a retailer, a utility-like service provider and a consumer-finance company. Its P&L can look profitable while it runs out of cash. That is why the model is built around **customer cohorts and the receivables book**, not around a simple sales P&L.

- **Formula evaluation.** All formulas were evaluated with the Python `formulas` engine (`tools/qa_evaluate.py`). There are 0 formula errors in Base and in Severe.
- **Independent shadow model.** `tools/shadow_shs.py` re-implements the logic in numpy. Excel and shadow agree exactly on annual revenue, net income, cash, gross receivables, facility balance and peak equity, in both Base and Severe.
- **Integrity checks.** The master check reads OK in Base and Severe.
