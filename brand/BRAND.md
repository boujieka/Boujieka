# Cartouche · African Bond Intelligence

## Name
**Cartouche** is the product brand; *African Bond Intelligence* is the descriptor.
In ancient Egypt a cartouche is the oval ring, tied at its base, that encloses a sovereign's
name. The platform's subject is sovereign debt, so the name and the mark follow from it.

Tagline (FR): *L'intelligence de la dette souveraine africaine.*
Tagline (EN): *Africa's sovereign debt, with its evidence.*

> Trademark availability has **not** been checked. Search the relevant registries
> (e.g. OAPI, ARIPO, WIPO Global Brand Database, EUIPO, USPTO) before public launch.

## Mark
`cartouche-mark.svg`: a lapis cartouche with a gold outline and gold base tie (the *shen*
bar). Inside are a gold sun disk and three faience obelisks rising left to right, which also
read as an upward yield curve.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `cartouche-mark.svg` | Mark alone (app icon, avatar, small spaces) |
| `cartouche-logo.svg` | Horizontal lockup: mark + wordmark + descriptor |
| `favicon.svg` | Browser tab, rounded lapis square |

Clear space: half the mark's width on every side. Minimum size: 16 px tall for the favicon,
32 px for the mark.

Data colours stay semantic: demand above its own history uses lapis, demand below uses
carnelian, and papyrus is the neutral midpoint. Colour never carries meaning alone: values and
▲▼ arrows are always printed.

## Type
- **Marcellus** (Google Fonts, OFL): wordmark and display headings, in inscriptional capitals.
- **IBM Plex Sans**: body text and UI.
- **IBM Plex Mono**: figures, tabular data, codes.

## Voice
Factual and calm. Neutral labels only ("High demand relative to recent auctions"), never
"best". Every number is marked FACT, CALC, ESTIMATE, AI or SYNTHETIC.
