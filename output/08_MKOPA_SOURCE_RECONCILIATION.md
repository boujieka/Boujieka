## 4. Conclusions

1. The primary filing provided cannot support the USD 416m figure, the USD 253.5m figure, or the net profit figures. It belongs to a UK subsidiary whose revenue is carbon credit sales.
2. The status of the M-KOPA revenue claim remains CONFLICTING SOURCES. It becomes resolvable only with the consolidated accounts of M-Kopa Holdings Limited (or another group entity identified as the reporting entity), read for entity, period, currency and revenue definition.
3. Until then:
   * the book should not quote either revenue figure as fact; at most it may say that secondary reports conflict and that the subsidiary filing reviewed does not report group revenue;
   * the model's Calibration reference built on SR01 and SR03 should be suspended (status NOT USED), and the "6.1 times" diagnostic removed from the book;
   * SR01 to SR04 should be re-graded D (unverified) with status CONFLICTING SOURCES, and a new entry should record the subsidiary filing as grade A, VERIFIED, NOT USED.
4. A point for the book itself: the case is a good illustration of the discipline Chapter 15 recommends. A figure reported as coming from "UK filings" turned out, on reading the filing at hand, to concern a different entity. The chapter can say this without naming the outlet's error, since the group filing has not been read.
