## Cover note

* This is an automated (AI) audit of rights and attribution, not a legal opinion. It inventories what the book reproduces and how it is attributed, and flags risk. Items marked **[COUNSEL]** must be confirmed by the author with a qualified adviser or with the rights holder before release.
* Scope: the 19 chapter sources in volumes/02-solar-home-systems/book/ (ch00_front.md, ch00b_intro.md, ch01.md to ch16.md, ch99_annexes.md; not volume2_book_draft.md and not the assembled book2_paygo_solar_finance_v0.2.md), the 15 figures in book/figures/, and the cover and copyright line produced by tools/publish_docs.py.
* Method: full text search of the sources for quotation marks, institution, document and company names; full reading of ch00, ch00b, ch07 and ch99; reading in context of every passage naming a third party; visual check of fig07 and metadata check of all 15 figures; comparison of the book's PERFORM tables with the held text of the 2026 and 2021 PERFORM guides (scratchpad/src/*.txt and the uploaded PDFs, pages 2 and 42 of the 2026 guide inspected as images).
* Network limits: gogla.org, cgap.org, lightingglobal.org, esmap.org, openknowledge.worldbank.org and creativecommons.org were all refused by the session's egress policy (WebFetch "EGRESS_BLOCKED"; curl 403 on CONNECT). No licence page could be opened on an official site. Licence findings are therefore either **read in the held document** (stated as such) or **secondary (search snippet)**, which is not the primary document and must be confirmed.
* The book was not modified. Nothing was committed.

## 1. Summary of findings

1. **No long quotation of third party text.** The book contains only two quoted external phrases, both of three words or fewer ("free token days", "at a stretch"). It reproduces no third party figure, logo, photograph or table image. All 15 figures are the author's own charts and diagrams (Matplotlib output from tools/build_book2_figures.py, drawn from the companion workbook).
2. **The main reuse is definitional:** the five PAYGo PERFORM 2026 KPIs, their formulas and six calculation rules are restated in a table in Chapter 7 (about 400 words) and again in Annex A Part 1 (about 210 words), with page references. The wording is the author's, but the selection and structure follow the guide's summary table (2026 guide, page 8). The held copy of the 2026 guide carries **no licence or copyright statement** (all 42 pages of extracted text searched; pages 2 and 42 viewed). Risk Medium until GOGLA confirms; this is the one item for which a permission or confirmation request is recommended.
3. **The 2021 PERFORM guide is CC BY 4.0** (read in the held document, page ii). Its definitions are restated, not copied, and are attributed by page. Attribution should be completed with the licence notice and the "not endorsed by CGAP/World Bank" adaptation wording.
4. **Factual statements about named companies** carry more risk than copyright. The highest is BBOXX: the book states that BBOXX LTD "is recorded as having entered administration on 19 May 2025", while the source register marks the Gazette notice and the Companies House entry as not read (Annex G, E7, Pending). Risk High for a statement about a named company's insolvency that rests on an unread primary record **[COUNSEL]**.
5. **One quotation is attributed to a document the project has not read**: "at a stretch" (ch02.md line 41), attributed to the ESMAP Market Trends Report 2024, whose primary PDF is not held. This breaks house rule 2 (quote only text read in the document). Easy fix.
6. **Copyright line and disclaimers are incomplete for a commercial edition:** the copyright line exists only on the cover (tools/publish_docs.py line 133) with a year that changes with each build; there is no copyright page, no third party licence notices, no trademark statement, no "no endorsement" statement and no "any resemblance" clause for SolaraPay and Kivara.
7. **Trademark use is descriptive throughout.** No logo is used, no institution or company is said to endorse, review or approve the book, the workbook or the method. Two refinements are proposed: a no endorsement statement (in particular because the workbook has a sheet named *PERFORM_2026*), and "IFRS Accounting Standards" in place of "under IFRS" (IFRS Foundation trade mark guidelines, secondary).
8. **"Solara Pay" name collision (low):** a web search finds an open source GitHub project called "Solara Pay" (stablecoin payroll on Solana, https://github.com/Don-Vicks/spay). Different field and spelling; no registered mark found by this search, but no formal trademark search was possible **[COUNSEL]**. "Kivara": no country or company of that exact name found (an Australian fashion brand "Kivari" exists).
9. Not referenced in the book: the PAYGo PERFORM Accounting Brief (October 2021), the Guidance for Company Analysis v3 beta (July 2025) and the Guidance for PAYGo RBF Funds (April 2026). They are held and registered as NOT USED; no rights issue arises in v0.2. Watu and Engie Energy Access are not named anywhere in the book.
