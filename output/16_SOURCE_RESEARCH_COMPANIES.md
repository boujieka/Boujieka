# Source research: company claims (Book 2 register E4 to E9 and benchmark database)

Prepared 3 October 2026 by an AI research agent. This is not a practitioner review and not a legal opinion. The register and the book were not changed.

## Method and its limit

Every primary host tried was refused by this session's network egress policy (HTTP 403 at the proxy, recorded as 'not reachable from this environment'): find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk, www.thegazette.co.uk, api.company-information.service.gov.uk, www.citigroup.com, sunking.com, www.m-kopa.com, www.bboxx.com, www.pkf-l.com, orrick.com, www.prnewswire.co.uk, and secondary hosts techcabal.com and dabafinance.com. Only a web search tool worked. All evidence below is therefore **secondary (search snippet)**: no primary document was opened and read, so **no claim is upgraded to VERIFIED**. Quotes are taken from search result summaries, not from the documents, and must be rechecked against the document before use.

What the research did achieve: it identified the primary documents and URLs to read, corroborated the figures across sources, corrected one register detail (the Gazette notice numbers for BBOXX) and narrowed the M-KOPA conflict.

## Key findings

1. **M-KOPA entity.** The ultimate parent named in the M-KOPA UK LIMITED filing, M-KOPA HOLDINGS LIMITED, is indexed at Companies House as company 10891868 (incorporated 31 July 2017, 27 Old Gloucester Street, London). The attribution in the benchmark files looks right but is not verified on the register. The consolidated FY2024 accounts were not read; the USD 416m against USD 253.5m conflict remains. KES 53.7bn cannot equal USD 253.5m at any 2024 exchange rate, so the outlets describe different lines, entities or currencies. An aggregator gives turnover GBP 185.8m for 10891868 for 2024, matching neither figure (unverified).
2. **BBOXX.** 19 May 2025 is corroborated as the administration date (administrator and Companies House insolvency page snippets). The appointment notice is Gazette 4891954 (published 22 May 2025); notice 4879301 is the winding up petition (presented 3 April 2025, hearing 21 May 2025). Last accounts made up to 31 December 2022; accounts overdue; administration extended (AM19, 1 May 2026).
3. **Sun King and d.light.** All figures are consistent with the company and bank releases as indexed, and the book's wording is accurate.
4. **ZOLA.** The USD 90m (45 and 45) round is consistent across TechCrunch and the counsel's announcement (Orrick).
