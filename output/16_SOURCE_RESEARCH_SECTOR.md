## Key findings

1. **E1-14 period wording at risk.** Snippets phrase the MTR figure as '62% in 2023, in line with 2021', a point value, not a 2021 to 2023 average. The book says 'average ... for 2021 to 2023' (ch01 l.112, ch07 l.69 and l.117, ch15 l.166, ch16 l.179). Recommend neutral wording until the PDF is read. Definition (deposits, weighting) still unknown.
2. **E1-15 quartiles unconfirmed and a competing dataset exists.** CGAP's PERFORM analysis is reported at median 72%, best quartile 79%, worst 54% (2022). Different sample and definition; must not be combined with the MTR 62%.
3. **E8-01: GOGLA's headline is USD 300m ('almost USD 300 million'), not USD 299m.** Decline of 30% corroborated. Recommend 'about USD 300m'. Report: GOGLA, After the Dip: Off-Grid Solar's Defining Moment (April 2025, as reported).
4. **E3-01, E10-01:** book wording consistent with secondary evidence; no numbers quoted; no change needed.
5. **MTR date:** October 2024 (PDF title as shown in search results; World Bank press release 8 Oct 2024).
