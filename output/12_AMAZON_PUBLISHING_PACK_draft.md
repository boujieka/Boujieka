## 1. Positioning

### 1.1 Core promise
**Proposed core promise:** "A practical financial framework for deciding whether a PAYGo solar company can survive growth, absorb credit losses and attract sustainable capital."

### 1.2 Where the book sits
The book sits where five subjects meet:
* PAYGo business models;
* credit risk;
* financial modelling;
* funding structures;
* investment committee analysis.

There is existing institutional literature on PAYGo, from CGAP, GOGLA, the PAYGo PERFORM initiative and ESMAP. The book should not be positioned against that literature. It should be positioned as the analyst's and investor's working method that uses it. Annex G already lists those sources.

### 1.3 Positioning claims to avoid
These are rules for the description, the cover and all marketing:
* No "investment grade", and no "bankable" used as a promise about any company.
* No figure whose source status is not VERIFIED. This rules out, for now:
  * the ESMAP 62% collection rate, until the report is held;
  * the M-KOPA revenue figures;
  * the Sun King and d.light transaction amounts.
* No statement that the model is tested in Excel until it is.
* No "the only", "the first" or "the definitive" book. These claims cannot be verified.

### 3.2 Trim size and page count
* The current PDF is A4, 117 pages.
* At 7 x 10 in, the page count is estimated at about 160 to 190. **Assumption:** this comes from the page area ratio (about 1.4) plus table and figure reflow.
* At 6 x 9 in, the estimate is about 210 to 240.
* **Recommendation:** use 7 x 10 in. Wide tables (the funding instrument table, the regulatory checklist, Annex A) stay readable without rotation.
* The page count is fixed only after the KDP interior is typeset.

### 3.3 Colour
* The 15 figures use a colour palette. KDP black-ink printing renders them in grey.
* Option A, black-ink interior: figures are redrawn so they read in greyscale, using line styles, markers, hatching and direct labels. This is the lowest print cost. The Kindle edition keeps colour.
* Option B, standard-colour interior: higher print cost (section 6), so the list price must rise or the royalty falls.
* **Recommendation:** Option A for the paperback. Hardcover colour options must be checked in KDP before choosing.

### 3.4 Kindle conversion
* The book has many tables. A reflowable Kindle shows wide tables poorly on phones.
* Options:
  * convert the widest tables to lists or images, with alt text;
  * use a fixed-layout or Print Replica edition.
* The eligibility and royalty treatment of the fixed-layout options must be checked in KDP before choosing.
* **Decision:** the author chooses after a test conversion.

### 3.5 ISBN
* The ebook needs no ISBN; Amazon assigns an ASIN.
* For print, KDP offers a free ISBN with "Independently published" as the imprint.
* An ISBN bought by the author allows the imprint "Africa Energy Finance". This is consistent with building a collection.
* **Decision.**

### 4.4 Description (draft, about 1,900 characters)
KDP descriptions have a length limit of about 4,000 characters; confirm it in the dashboard.

> **How do you know whether a PAYGo solar company is profitable, generates cash and can be financed?**
>
> A PAYGo solar company is three businesses in one legal entity: a hardware retailer, a utility like service provider and a consumer lender. Each earns money differently, and each can fail differently. Revenue booked at the point of sale can sit beside a cash position that will not recover for more than a year.
>
> *PAYGo Solar Finance* gives a practical framework for analysing all three. It follows the chain an investor or lender actually has to check: customer and affordability, product and price plan, cohort repayment, credit losses, receivables, cash, foreign exchange, funding, results based financing, stress testing, valuation and the investment committee decision.
>
> Inside the book:
> * Read PAYGo unit economics after acquisition, servicing and credit losses
> * Analyse cohorts and repayment curves using the PAYGo PERFORM 2026 KPIs
> * Model credit losses, receivables and the borrowing base
> * Structure equity, debt, receivables facilities and securitisation
> * Separate results based financing from the commercial case
> * Stress test the business and decide go, conditional go or stop
>
> Sixteen chapters and eight annexes include a KPI dictionary, a credit policy template, an investor due diligence checklist, an investment memo template and a receivables facility term sheet checklist. A fictional company, SolaraPay, is used throughout to show the method on one consistent set of numbers.
>
> Written for founders and CFOs, banks and development finance institutions, impact investors and fund managers, programme managers and energy access professionals.
>
> The book is Book 2 of the Africa Energy Finance collection. A companion financial model, user manual and case study are available separately.

**Notes on the description:**
* The last sentence may only be used once the companion products are actually available (gates 2 and 3).
* Formatting must use only the limited HTML that KDP accepts.

## 8. Reviewers, endorsements and Amazon reviews

### 8.1 Peer review panel (v0.3)
* Proposed panel:
  * five PAYGo practitioners;
  * three investors;
  * two lenders or DFIs;
  * two energy access programme specialists;
  * two financial modellers.
* Each signs a short confidentiality note and returns comments on a template, with chapter, page, issue, proposed fix and severity.
* Comments are logged in output/10_PUBLICATION_QA_CHECKLIST.

### 8.2 Endorsements
* Short quotes, with written permission and the person's chosen title and affiliation, for the cover and the "Editorial reviews" section in KDP.
* An endorsement must not state or imply that an institution endorses the book unless the institution itself has agreed.

### 8.3 Amazon customer reviews
* Amazon's community guidelines restrict reviews from people with a close personal or financial relationship to the author.
* Peer reviewers who are acknowledged in the book, or who were paid, should give endorsements, not customer reviews.
* Advance copies may be given to readers, but no review may be required in exchange, and no positive review may be asked for.
* Read the current guideline text before the launch. Breaches can lead to reviews being removed.

## 10. KDP Select

**Do not enrol at launch.** KDP Select requires the ebook to be exclusive to Amazon for each enrolment period (KDP, verified). That exclusivity would prevent:
* selling the PDF from the Africa Energy Finance site;
* bundling the ebook with the model;
* institutional PDF licences.

Select can be reconsidered later if those channels turn out to be unused.
