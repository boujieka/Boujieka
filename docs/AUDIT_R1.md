## Open issues for the author

1. LIT:S2 (Hydro Finance Handbook) remains a search summary only; a web search found nothing more. Either obtain the document or drop it from Table P.1.
2. *Bankable Is Not Enough* (front:83) and the earlier policy paper carry no source identifier; add a bibliographic entry if they are published.
3. Narrative timing: the model's development years run 2021 to 2026 with close in 2027, so "the start of permitting" is 2024 in model time, while the book is written in 2026 and Tamarind is described as at that stage now. Consider stating the case date explicitly.
4. Kasiri's capacity factor (55.6%) is above the IRENA African large-hydro average (48%). Defensible as illustrative, but a reviewer may ask why.
5. HY-01's database row (source_database.md) lists only large-hydro capacity factors; add the small-hydro values (Table 6.5: 50/55/66) and IRENA's 10 MW size boundary.
6. CL-04 is marked S in the database; I read the page (now P), and the ZRA press release [R1:S1] confirms 16 bcm. The 2023 figure of 30 bcm still rests on news reporting.
7. Outside my files: Ch15 has "about 6.0x times the PPA bill" (double "times").
8. DE:S14, S15, S16, S39b, S53 were accepted on the evidence file's record and not re-read.
