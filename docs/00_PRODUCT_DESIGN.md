## 1. The question the product answers

> **Can this project actually deliver bankable power without creating unsustainable public liabilities?**

Core philosophy: *Technical feasibility is not financial bankability. Financial bankability is not sustainable public finance.*

## 2. Market gap (summary of `research/competitive_intelligence.md`)

The competitive research reviewed 33 products: textbooks, commercial Excel models, training courses, and free institutional tools. What it found:

| Product category | What it covers well | What it does not integrate |
|---|---|---|
| Project finance textbooks (e.g. Gatti 4th ed. 2023, Yescombe, Finnerty) | Generic PF structuring, debt sizing, contracts | Hydrology, African utility viability, fiscal exposure |
| Commercial hydro / renewable Excel templates (verified list prices about US$45–499; one hydro model US$109 discounted / US$149 list) | Generation, simple returns | Transmission, utility ability to pay, guarantees, public finance. Several are 5-year models with no DSCR sizing listed |
| PF modelling courses (WSP US$499; CFI US$497/847 per year; Forvis Mazars US$3,200 for 2 days; LCT £2,700–4,495) | Modelling technique | Sector and fiscal content |
| Free institutional tools (IMF/World Bank PFRAM 2.0; LIC-DSF; World Bank UPBEAT; Kojima & Trimble quasi-fiscal deficit work; ALSF and Power Africa handbooks; IHA/HSS tools; IFC hydro developer guide) | Each covers one layer in depth | Not linked to each other or to a project cash-flow engine |

**Gap (analyst inference):** none of the reviewed products links hydrology, transmission, the offtaker's ability to pay, PPA design, project finance, PPP structure choice, contingent liabilities and fiscal screening in one auditable engine with explicit bankability gates. Bankable Hydro sits in that intersection and is built to feed PFRAM and LIC-DSF work, not replace it.

## 6. Original intellectual property

1. **Hydropower Bankability Framework™.** Nine gates (the brief asked for at least eight; Gate 9 covers E&S and sustainability). Each gate has 2–3 metric tests with three explicit thresholds. The gate status is the weakest test, so there is no hidden weighting. *Trademark status: the ™ symbol is a claim of use only; no registration has been checked or filed.*
2. **Offtaker Payment Capacity Gap.** This compares the PPA bill against a utility's maximum sustainable PPA payment: cash available before the new PPA, divided by a coverage buffer.
3. **Transmission Readiness Gap.** The timing gap (transmission COD minus plant COD) and the capacity gap (installed MW minus evacuation MW at COD). Both are tied into deemed energy and therefore into the utility and fiscal layers.
4. **Shortfall routing.** A utility shortfall is routed to either a budget backstop, a sovereign PPA guarantee call, or unpaid arrears to the IPP. The model shows which party ends up holding the risk in each scenario.
5. **Project-Level Fiscal Exposure Screening.** It is labelled as a screen and explicitly does not replace a DSA.
6. **Same-project structure comparison.** Five structures are run through both a closed-form screen and the full engine.

## 7. Pricing hypothesis (NOT demand-tested)

The verified competitor price points above suggest three tiers (analyst inference only):

- Book: comparable to professional finance titles.
- Model + manual: well above template marketplaces (US$45–499), because of the integration and institutional audience.
- Course / cohort training: within the range of specialist PF courses (US$500–4,500 per seat).

A seat price of roughly US$500–3,000 for institutional buyers was suggested in the research. This needs validation through customer interviews before launch.

## 8. Known limitations and roadmap

| Limitation (v1.0) | Planned improvement |
|---|---|
| Annual periodicity; proportional curtailment | Monthly dispatch module for storage/peaking plants |
| Normal-approximation P75/P90 | Import of simulated annual energy series; empirical percentiles |
| Single-utility simplified cash model | Optional multi-year utility balance sheet; portfolio of PPAs |
| Snapshot tables need LibreOffice/Python | Optional VBA button version for Excel-only users |
| Deterministic guarantee calls; user-judgement probabilities | Optional Monte-Carlo add-in |
| Portfolio fiscal view (Lao lesson: aggregate all PPAs) | "Portfolio" sheet that sums several projects' commitments |
